# Native lyric craft — v1.7

Review Chinese in isolation before comparison to English. Test: syntactic completeness; semantic chunking and antecedent clarity; natural verb-object and preposition pairing; style consistent with the entire song; first-hearing comprehension; content words on strong notes; vowel sostenuto practicality; register distinction between solemn hymn diction and English calque. Do not turn every hymn into casual speech. Do not introduce new doctrinal propositions merely for rhyme.

## Representative negative fixtures

Invented illustrations of categories. They are not rulings on any real song, and wording a user has accepted is not re-flagged because it resembles one of them.

- `为这土地，为我们收成`: possessive 的 dropped for count (`我们收成`); count equality does not pass. Check whether the ellipsis is a deliberate poetic compression before flagging.
- `奉差遣为见证`: unnatural verb-purpose linkage; compare `被差遣来作见证`.
- `万物诸天独一王`: compressed nominal ordering; seek a natural relational phrase.
- `盼望信靠与平安`: bare noun stack may obscure the referent; assess whether a poetic triad is intended.
- `同属一个家`: possible collective-unity paraphrase of a source "one family"; do not reject solely for lacking a literal `一`. Check register and context.

## Prosodic uncertainty

Word count is not onset count, and a matched count does not validate lexical stress, Mandarin tone, open vowels or congregational singing. A longer candidate earns no automatic approval from theoretical subdivisibility. Show the bar/note allocation or mark pending.
